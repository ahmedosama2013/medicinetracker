# 💊 MedicalTracker

**MedicalTracker** is a clean, dark theme, senior-friendly medicine tracking web and mobile application built to help you stay on top of your daily medicines, build lasting healthy habits, and monitor your consistency over time.

## ✨ Features

* **Daily Tracking:** Easily log ☀️ Day and 🌙 Night medicines with real-time visual progress indicators.
* **Custom Medicines:** Add, edit, and delete your own medicines with a name and dose.
* **Interactive Calendar:** View past completion history using circular progress rings with full support to edit past entries.
* **Overview Statistics:** Track total doses taken, monthly completion rates, perfect days, and best streaks.
* **Senior-Friendly Design:** Large text, high contrast, and big touch-friendly buttons for easy everyday use.
* **Privacy First:** All data is saved directly in your browser or device storage.
* **Web App:** Can also be downloaded as a web app.
