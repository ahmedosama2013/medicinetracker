Welcome to MedicineTracker!

A simple, daily companion to help you stay on top of your medicines.

  • • Add Medicines: Tap "Add Medicine" and choose Day or Night.
  • • Mark as Taken: Tap a medicine on the "Today" tab when you take it.
  • • Review History: Use the "Calendar" tab to check or fix past days.
  • • Saved Automatically: Everything is stored on this device, safely.
